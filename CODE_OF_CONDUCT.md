# Code of conduct

We want DAGrail participation to be safe, constructive, and technically rigorous.

Be respectful, assume good intent while examining evidence, welcome different levels of
experience, and criticize ideas or changes rather than people. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication
of another person's private information are unacceptable.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, community spaces, and public
representation of the project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close
threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid
retaliation against good-faith reporters.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub Security Advisory
channel described in SECURITY.md. If the maintainer is involved, use GitHub's platform
reporting tools. Reports will be handled with discretion; absolute confidentiality
cannot be guaranteed when safety or law requires escalation.
